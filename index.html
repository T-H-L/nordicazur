The business uses a tiered subscription framework supplemented by an à la carte menu to secure baseline cash flow through the off-season while capturing high margin during summer.

Essential Inspection

€130 – €180 / month

(Min. 6- or 12-month commitment)

  • 2 physical inspections per month.
  • Mail collection & urgent forwarding (To prevent a vacant house appearance).
  • Post-storm visual check (after major regional weather alerts. Checks are triggered by official weather alerts e.g. Meteo France orange/rouge warning).
  • Utility cycling (flushing toilets/taps to avoid sewer odors; running AC/heating brief test).
  • Visual check of grounds/pool status (detecting leaks, equipment faults, green water).
  • Digital photo status report sent after each visit.
  • Secure keyholding.
  • Perimeter & Security Integrity check. (Inspect gates, fences, doors, windows and shutters for signs of forced entry/ vandalism).
  • Utility meter readings. (water & electricity). Log meter numbers during visits to flag hidden plumbing leaks or unexpected power drains early.
  • Security & Alarm systems visual check. Verify that security keypads, motion detectors and camera indicator lights are active and functioning.

Comfort Home

(Min. 6-month commitment)

  • 4 inspections per month (weekly cadence).
  • All checks from Essential Inspection included.
